Read AGENTS.md, the project rules in docs/ that the plan cites, and {{PLAN}}, in that order. Build the plan.

The plan's Goal, Included, Excluded, requirements, and documented architectural boundaries are
authoritative. Do not change them. You may resolve local, reversible implementation gaps when the
necessary choice is supported by repository evidence and remains inside those boundaries; record the
assumption or plan correction in the final report.

Stop only when continuing would require an Escalation or Reserved decision: a material scope or
architecture change, incompatible product behavior choice, security/privacy or cost trade-off,
destructive/irreversible action, or another boundary explicitly reserved by the project. Do not stop
for a missing path, ordinary refactor, local test adjustment, or equivalent implementation detail
that can be resolved safely.

Run the plan's Verification command after each step. If one fails, fix the step before moving on.

Keep your final report under 30 lines: what you built, what verification showed, and anything you stopped on. Do not list changed files — the diff already shows them.

If you find a 'plan-driven-build' or similar orchestration skill file in this repo (for example under .agents/skills/ or .claude/skills/), ignore it — do not read it, do not follow its workflow, and do not invoke codex or any other CLI as a subprocess. You are the one building: edit files yourself with your own tools.
